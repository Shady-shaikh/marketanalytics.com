Investor Redressal Framework

Complaints Redressal

A transparent, ethical, and time-bound process to address investor complaints as per SEBI Research Analyst guidelines.

Clients’ queries / complaints may arise due to lack of understanding or a deficiency of service experienced by clients. Deficiency of service may include lack of explanation, clarifications, or understanding which escalates into shortfalls in the expected delivery standards.

1
Seek Clarification / Raise Initial Query

Clients can seek clarification to their query and are entitled to make a complaint in writing, orally or telephonically.

Escalation to Research Analyst

Clients can write to the Research Analyst if they do not receive a response within 10 business days of writing to the Client Servicing Team.

SEBI SCORES Portal

If not satisfied with the response, clients may lodge their grievance with SEBI via the centralized web portal SCORES:
