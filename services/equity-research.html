Who is this service for?

Equity Research is suitable for investors who prefer delivery-based or positional equity trades with a medium to long-term horizon and are willing to accept market risk without expecting fixed or guaranteed returns.

  • Delivery-based equity investors and positional traders
  • Clients comfortable holding positions beyond 1–3 months
  • Investors seeking research-driven decisions, not speculative tips

What You Receive

  • ✔ Research-backed stock ideas with defined entry, review & risk levels
  • ✔ Technical and fundamental rationale for each recommendation
  • ✔ Positional, swing & medium-term opportunity tracking
  • ✔ Risk grading and expected holding horizon
  • ✔ Portfolio-level risk and concentration commentary (research view)

What We Do NOT Do

  • ✘ No execution of trades on your behalf
  • ✘ No intraday leverage or margin funding
  • ✘ No assured, fixed, or minimum returns
  • ✘ No portfolio management or fund management

Our Research Process

  • ✔ Screening universe based on liquidity, compliance & risk filters
  • ✔ Technical analysis using trend, volume & price structure
  • ✔ Selective integration of fundamental & earnings parameters
  • ✔ Ongoing review and update notes when market regimes change
