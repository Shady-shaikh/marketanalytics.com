SEBI Mandated Document

Investor Charter – Research Analyst (RA)

This Investor Charter defines the rights, responsibilities, and services available to investors engaging with SEBI-Registered Research Analysts, ensuring transparency, ethics, and investor protection.

A Vision and Mission for Investors

Vision

Invest with knowledge, transparency, and confidence.

Mission

To empower investors through unbiased, research-led insights that support informed decision-making and long-term financial well-being.

B Details of Business Transacted by Research Analyst

  • Preparation and publication of research reports supported by data and analysis.
  • Providing research recommendations based on public information and analytical models.
  • Maintaining records of research activities, communications, and justifications.
  • Disclosing conflicts of interest related to recommendations.
  • Ensuring transparency in methodology, ratings, and assumptions.
  • Ensuring research reports meet SEBI-mandated disclosures.
  • Annual audit and regulatory compliance as prescribed by SEBI.
  • No access to investor funds or securities — RA provides research only.

C Details of Services Provided to Investors

Research-Related Services

  • Publishing research reports and recommendations.
  • Providing rationale, methodology, and assumptions.
  • Providing risk ratings, targets, and timelines.
  • Timely updates on recommendation changes.

Disclosure Obligations

  • Disclosure of conflicts of interest, if any.
  • Disclosure of ownership or financial interest.
  • Clear declaration of non-involvement in execution or PMS.

Investor Protection

  • No guaranteed or assured returns.
  • No inducements or pressure-based recommendations.
  • Clear risk warnings for volatile securities.

D Grievance Redressal Mechanism

1. Raise Complaint with Research Analyst

Investors may first contact the Research Analyst directly via official channels (grievance@solvitasanalytics.com). The RA shall respond within 30 days.

2. SEBI SCORES Platform

If unresolved, complaints may be escalated via: https://scores.sebi.gov.in

3. Physical Complaint Submission

Office of Investor Assistance and Education, Securities and Exchange Board of India, SEBI Bhavan, Plot No. C4-A, G Block, Bandra Kurla Complex, Bandra (E), Mumbai – 400051.

E Rights of Investors

  • Right to unbiased and independent research.
  • Right to transparency regarding conflicts of interest.
  • Right to risk disclosures and rationale.
  • Right to timely updates and revisions.
  • Right to privacy and confidentiality.
  • Right to clear and non-misleading communication.
  • Right to grievance redressal timelines.
  • Protection against guaranteed-return schemes.

F Responsibilities of Investors (Do’s & Don’ts)

1. Do’s

  • Verify SEBI registration of the Research Analyst.
  • Read all disclosures carefully.
  • Understand risks before investing.
  • Maintain records of communications.
  • Use official channels only.
  • Report misleading claims to SEBI.

2. Don’ts

  • Do not believe in guaranteed returns.
  • Do not share trading or banking credentials.
  • Do not act solely on WhatsApp/Telegram tips.
  • Do not transfer funds for trading to the Research Analyst.
  • Do not rely on rumors or unverified advice.
