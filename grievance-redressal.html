SEBI Compliance & Investor Protection

Grievance Redressal Process

A transparent and structured grievance redressal framework designed to ensure fairness, responsiveness, and complete investor protection under SEBI regulations.

A Welcome Note

Solvitas Analytics is committed to maintaining the highest standards of integrity, transparency, and investor satisfaction. We ensure that all investor grievances are addressed in a structured, fair, and time-bound manner as required under SEBI Research Analyst Regulations.

This Grievance Redressal Policy outlines the principles and mechanisms through which investors can raise concerns and receive resolutions promptly.

B Core Principles of Our Grievance Redressal

  • Investors will be treated fairly and courteously at all times.
  • All grievances will be addressed promptly within regulatory timelines.
  • Complaints will be handled in a transparent, unbiased, and efficient manner.
  • Research staff will act in good faith without prejudice toward investors.
  • Continuous efforts will be made to eliminate recurring issues.

C Grievance Redressal Mechanism

Investors may raise complaints for issues such as service deficiencies, clarifications required, non-receipt of reports, or any conduct-related concerns. All communications should be made through official channels only.

Step 1: Contact RA

Reach out using official grievance contacts. Response within 30 days.

Step 2: SEBI SCORES

If unresolved, lodge complaint on scores.sebi.gov.in.

Step 3: Physical Complaint

Submit written complaints to SEBI Head Office in BKC, Mumbai.
